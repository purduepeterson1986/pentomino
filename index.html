Pentomino

Fit all 12 pieces into the 6 × 10 tray. There are 2,339 different ways to do it. How many can you find?

Game

Pieces placed for you

The game picks a solution you haven't found yet and pins some of its pieces in place. Fewer pinned pieces makes a harder puzzle.

Options

Live checkShows whether the pieces on the board can still lead to a full solution, and how many ways remain.
Letters on piecesShows each piece's name (F, I, L, N, P, T, U, V, W, X, Y, Z).
SoundClicks when pieces are picked up and placed, and a fanfare for each solution.
